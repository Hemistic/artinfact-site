01

Prepare your sources

01

Workspaces & bookshelves

Keep papers and notes together by research topic. Browse titles, authors and reading material in a shared workspace, turning scattered files into a research library you can keep building.

02

Import your research material

Bring local PDFs, Markdown notes, images and editable whiteboard packages into one workspace. Keep original papers, figures and your own records close at hand for reading and reuse.

03

MinerU parsing & source cards

Convert a paper into Markdown, structured data, figures and source coordinates. Prepare material for navigation and source cards without automatically asking an Agent to interpret it.

04

A library of source material

Browse and search full texts, sections, figures, equations, annotations and references. Choose the cards to bring onto your board while retaining their connection to the source.

02

Read & annotate

05

An original-page PDF reader

Read original pages with the integrated Zotero PDF reader. Navigate, zoom, search and inspect the document structure, with annotations and outlines alongside it and reading state stored locally.

06

Annotations that stay with your reading

Capture highlights, underlines, notes and selected regions. Keep useful passages and figures together with your own observations, ready to revisit or use as context for a question.

07

AI outlines & chapter context

Request a navigable outline grounded in the paper, with a short explanation of each section. Get a view of the argument before choosing where to read more closely.

08

Insights with supporting evidence

Organize contributions, experimental evidence and limitations into source-linked insights. Return to the original passage to check a conclusion, its assumptions and the conditions of an experiment.

03

Analyze & explain

09

Summarize, translate & refine a card

Work on the material you choose: organize a card, summarize it, extract key points, translate it or give a custom research instruction. Review proposed rewrites before accepting them where the editor offers that workflow.

10

Interpret figures, tables & equations

Read a figure or table in the context of the paper, or work through an equation step by step. Keep original evidence separate from further explanation; related explanatory images depend on host capabilities.

11

Research across connected cards

Use connections to express dependencies and background relationships. Give a comparison a clear scope by linking the cards or papers whose methods and evidence you want to examine.

12

Research within a region

Group related material around a common question. Request analysis of that region and keep resulting cards in the research workspace, gradually building a source-backed line of argument.

04

Organize knowledge

13

Multiple boards, different perspectives

Create a board for each question. Arrange cards with dragging, resizing, colored regions and connections, using the same material to develop different research views.

14

Editable Markdown notes

Write and revise headings, lists, tasks, quotations, images, tables, code and mathematics with Milkdown. Develop reading notes into structured documents while edits are saved locally.

15

A path back to the PDF

Keep passages, paper identities and page references beside your analysis. Jump back to the original context and distinguish what the source says from what you infer.

16

A Wiki & connected knowledge graph

Build Wiki pages and links around sources, concepts, evidence, conditions and questions. Explore relationships across papers in a dynamic graph to look for shared mechanisms and research boundaries.

05

Build on your work

17

Linked Pages & native editing

Save or link notes to Pages through the connected integration and continue editing in the original page. Synchronize titles and summaries when requested while retaining a local copy of the source text.

18

Export documents & editable boards

Organize a board into a Markdown document with its images and resources, or export an editable board package. Continue working with the results outside the canvas or organize them in Pages.

19

Follow a paper’s references

Start with an abstract-based explanation of a referenced paper, then choose deeper reading when the full text is available. Keep the reading scope explicit so an abstract summary is never mistaken for a full-paper review.

20

Templates for thoughtful reading

Prepare a structure around the research question, method, evidence and limitations. Use it to guide your own notes, then bring the material you need onto the whiteboard and continue developing it.

AI participates when you choose

Basic parsing and Agent research are separate. Pages, AI generation and downloads depend on connected hosts or services. Check parsing and generated explanations against the source.
