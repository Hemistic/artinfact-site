00:00 — ARTINFACT | COMPLETE FEATURE TOUR

Artinfact brings original PDFs, annotations, Markdown notes, whiteboards and a knowledge graph into one research workspace. This tour introduces every major feature. Recorded scenes include the desktop plugin and a local preview of the same build. AI sections explain the available workflows and controls.

00:21 — 01 WORKSPACES & BOOKSHELVES

Keep papers and notes together by research topic. Browse titles, authors and reading material in a shared workspace, turning scattered files into a research library you can keep building.

00:34 — 02 IMPORT YOUR RESEARCH MATERIAL

Bring local PDFs, Markdown notes, images and editable whiteboard packages into one workspace. Keep original papers, figures and your own records close at hand for reading and reuse.

00:48 — 03 MINERU PARSING & SOURCE CARDS

Miner U prepares Markdown, structured data, figures, tables, equations and source coordinates from a PDF. This is automatic source preparation. The source used in this tour was parsed beforehand. The reader can open original PDF pages independently, while parsing and other preparation happen separately.

01:10 — 04 A LIBRARY OF SOURCE MATERIAL

Browse and search full texts, sections, figures, equations, annotations and references. Choose the cards to bring onto your board while retaining their connection to the source.

01:23 — 05 AN ORIGINAL-PAGE PDF READER

Read original pages with the integrated Zotero PDF reader. Navigate, zoom, search and inspect the document structure, with annotations and outlines alongside it and reading state stored locally.

01:37 — 06 ANNOTATIONS THAT STAY WITH YOUR READING

Capture highlights, underlines, notes and selected regions. Keep useful passages and figures together with your own observations, ready to revisit or use as context for a question.

01:50 — 07 AI OUTLINES & CHAPTER CONTEXT

AI Notes in the reader outline sidebar requests a section-by-section outline grounded in the paper. Each section can include a short explanation to help you decide where to read next. The same outline entry is available in the source preparation menu. An outline updates navigation rather than creating a whiteboard card.

02:12 — 08 INSIGHTS WITH SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

Request AI insights and annotations when you want help interpreting the paper. The agent reads the available source material, develops insights with supporting evidence, and saves the result through MCP. Saved insights appear under the PDF in the plus library, ready for you to choose and add to a board. This scene shows the entry points.

02:35 — 09 SUMMARIZE, TRANSLATE & REFINE A CARD

Work on the material you choose: organize a card, summarize it, extract key points, translate it or give a custom research instruction. Review proposed rewrites before accepting them where the editor offers that workflow.

02:51 — 10A READ AND RECOGNIZE TABLES

Table cards retain the original table image and its caption. The research menu can request an explanation of indicators, results and comparison conditions. It also offers recognition as a Markdown table. The intended result keeps the editable explanation or table above the original image, so you can compare the interpretation with its source.

03:14 — 10B UNDERSTAND FORMULAS

Formula cards render the original mathematical expression. Their research menu offers explanations of symbols, step-by-step derivation, original definitions and applicable assumptions. You can also enter your own question. These are user-requested research actions, with the source context available to the agent.

03:36 — 10C INTERPRET FIGURES

Figure cards pair the original image with its caption and notes. Ask for the process, mechanism, figure description or translation. When the host supports it, an explanatory image can also be requested. You choose the instruction and send it when you want the agent to participate.

03:56 — 11 RESEARCH ACROSS CONNECTED CARDS

Use connections to express dependencies and background relationships. Give a comparison a clear scope by linking the cards or papers whose methods and evidence you want to examine.

04:08 — 12 RESEARCH WITHIN A REGION

Group related material around a common question. Request analysis of that region and keep resulting cards in the research workspace, gradually building a source-backed line of argument.

04:20 — 13 MULTIPLE BOARDS, DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES

Create a board for each question. Arrange cards with dragging, resizing, colored regions and connections, using the same material to develop different research views.

04:32 — 14 EDITABLE MARKDOWN NOTES

Milkdown provides headings, lists, tasks, tables, code, mathematics and inline HTML. Notes also support highlighted text, subscripts, superscripts and Mermaid diagrams. Select a card to edit. Table alignment controls appear beside the cell, and code blocks show their language. Long content stays within the card and can be scrolled.

05:00 — 15 A PATH BACK TO THE PDF

Keep passages, paper identities and page references beside your analysis. Use the original PDF page to verify the context, and distinguish what the source says from what you infer.

05:13 — 16 A WIKI & CONNECTED KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

The knowledge graph is built from real Wiki pages and explicit links. Nodes represent sources, concepts, questions and other knowledge. Drag a node to explore its neighborhood, then open its details to inspect related information or add it to a board. Graph settings control layout spacing, soft or standard drag rebound, label visibility and font size. You can also zoom and change the theme.

05:41 — 17 LINKED PAGES & NATIVE EDITING

Link a note to an existing Pages page using its original link or page ID. With the Pages integration connected, you can continue editing in the original page and synchronize its title and summary when requested. Local note text remains available. This scene shows the association interface; it does not create or synchronize an external page.

06:04 — 18 EXPORT DOCUMENTS & EDITABLE BOARDS

Export the board as a Markdown document with its image resources, or as an editable whiteboard package with a JSON Canvas file. Regions become document sections, and connections help determine the order of cards. Folding does not shorten the exported text. Here, the exported board contains nine cards, two connections, one region and three image resources.

06:30 — 19 FOLLOW A PAPER’S REFERENCES

Reference reading has two levels. Start with an abstract interpretation to understand what a cited paper is about. Choose deeper reading when the full text is available. If needed, enable the option to download its PDF into the current workspace. You decide when to send the request. An abstract summary and a full-paper reading have different evidence scopes.

06:55 — 20 TEMPLATES FOR THOUGHTFUL READING

Reading templates provide a starting structure for the research question, method, evidence chain and limitations. Open them from the plus library or the source preparation menu. Choose the structure you need, then develop your own observations and notes. Automatic preparation supplies the material, while research instructions run when you choose to send them.

07:18 — YOUR RESEARCH, READY TO REUSE

Artinfact connects reading, evidence and your own notes. Start from the original source, choose where AI can help, and take your work with you. Artinfact is free to use, by Hemistic.
