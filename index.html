An original-page PDF reader

Read original pages with the integrated Zotero PDF reader. Navigate, zoom, search and inspect the document structure, with annotations and outlines alongside it and reading state stored locally.

Explore this feature →

Editable Markdown notes

Write and revise headings, lists, tasks, quotations, images, tables, code and mathematics with Milkdown. Develop reading notes into structured documents while edits are saved locally.

Explore this feature →

Interpret figures, tables & equations

Read a figure or table in the context of the paper, or work through an equation step by step. Keep original evidence separate from further explanation; related explanatory images depend on host capabilities.

Explore this feature →

Research within a region

Group related material around a common question. Request analysis of that region and keep resulting cards in the research workspace, gradually building a source-backed line of argument.

Explore this feature →

A Wiki & connected knowledge graph

Build Wiki pages and links around sources, concepts, evidence, conditions and questions. Explore relationships across papers in a dynamic graph to look for shared mechanisms and research boundaries.

Explore this feature →

Export documents & editable boards

Organize a board into a Markdown document with its images and resources, or export an editable board package. Continue working with the results outside the canvas or organize them in Pages.
