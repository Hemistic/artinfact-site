Privacy policy

Updated and effective: October 24, 2026

This policy describes data handling by the Artinfact plugin and this website, maintained by Hemistic. Contact: 414482116@qq.com.

1. Local data and permissions

PDFs, notes, annotations, parsing outputs, whiteboards, images and exports are primarily stored in your selected local library, normally Artinfact inside your system Documents folder. Settings, reading state and diagnostics may be stored separately in local plugin data. Cloud synchronization such as OneDrive follows your system configuration.

The local MCP service uses permissions granted by its host. Authorize only files you are entitled to process. Basic card preparation extracts source material; it does not automatically generate AI opinions.

2. External processing

FeatureData and recipient
AI researchWhen requested, the host Agent can read relevant cards, passages, images or full text. The host and its model services process this context.
Selection translationWhen enabled, selected text and the target language are sent to the chosen Bing/Google service. Some historical versions enabled this by default; check reader settings. Saved preferences are retained.
PagesAuthorized creation, linking, synchronization or export shares text, images and page metadata with the connected Pages service.
Models and papersDownloads contact dependency sources, ModelScope/Hugging Face or selected paper websites, which receive requests and ordinary network metadata.

Local storage does not mean every feature is offline. Independent services apply their own policies, account requirements and regional rules. Processing may cross borders depending on the services you select.

3. Telemetry, website and support

Hemistic does not separately collect plugin telemetry or user documents, or operate advertising, marketing tracking or payment collection. This static website has no analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support email sent to the QQ mailbox may include your email address, description and attachments, used only to handle your request. Support messages are deleted within 90 days after closure unless legally required to be retained. Do not send credentials or unauthorized documents.

4. Retention and deletion

You manage local data, which normally remains until you delete it. Local diagnostics can contain timestamps, versions, events, errors and file paths; there is no uniform automatic retention period. Removing a card from a board does not delete its shared note, and archives, attachments and exports can remain.

Check the library, archives, settings, logs, exports, backups, Pages and host conversations separately when deleting data. Uninstallation does not imply deletion of all copies. Manage third-party copies through the respective service.

5. Choices, requests and updates

You can disable translation, avoid AI commands and Pages connections, and manage your local files. Contact the support address for access, correction or deletion requests concerning support email; confirmation that you control the originating address may be needed. This policy does not limit applicable statutory rights.

Changes are published on this page with an updated date. Age and regional eligibility also depend on the host platform and connected services.
